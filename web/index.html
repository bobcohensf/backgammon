How to Play:

  1. Click "New Game" to start a new game
  2. Click "Roll Dice" to roll the dice for the current player
  3. Click on a checker to select it (valid destinations will be highlighted)
  4. Click on a highlighted destination to move the checker
  5. Click "Reset Move" to undo all moves and try again
  6. Click "Accept Move" to complete your turn
  7. Player 1 (X) moves UP (1→24), Player 2 (O) moves DOWN (24→1)
